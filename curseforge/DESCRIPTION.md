# Amity Toolkit – a quality-of-life toolkit for WoW Forever

Hi - I am Amity, I've been playing WoW since Vanilla day 1, mainly Warrior/Rogue/Mage/DH. This is just a QoL toolkit with features that the game is (currently) missing and/or that make life easier.

As I plan to play WoW Forever heavily, this Addon will be kept up-to-date!

Have an idea for a module, or found a bug?

## ✨ Current modules

### 🩸 Bar Text
Always shows health and resource values on the **player**, **target** and **target-of-target** frames.

- Formats: **`100/200 50%`**, **`100/200`** or **`50%`**
- Adjustable font size

### 💰 Auction Item Level
Adds a sortable **iLvl column** to the auction house browse results, colored by item quality.

### 🛡️ LFG Role Filter
**Tank / Healer / DPS** buttons in the Looking For Group browse list.

- Solo players are matched by the roles they can fill
- Groups are matched by the roles they still need or already have
- Counter shows how many results match

### ⚔️ Overpower Alert - Warrior only
Large Overpower icon with a **5-second countdown** when your target dodges.

- Also in **Berserker Stance** when you play solo, so you can switch to Battle Stance in time

### 🗡️ Revenge Alert - Warrior only
Large Revenge icon with a **5-second countdown** when you block, dodge or parry.

### 📣 Battle Shout Alert - Warrior only
Large **flashing** icon while Battle Shout is missing or about to run out.

- Warns 30 seconds before it ends by default (adjustable)
- Optional: only warn in combat

**All Warrior alerts** can be moved on their own, have their own alert sound and a test button, and are tinted blue while you lack the rage.

### 🕒 Chat Timestamps
The time in front of every chat mess[DESCRIPTION.md](DESCRIPTION.md)age, e.g. `14:17`.

- 24 or 12 hour format, with or without seconds
- Optional brackets and gray color

### ✨ Auto Replace Enchant - off by default
Skips the **"replace this enchant?"** prompt, handy for levelling Enchanting.

- Careful: a good enchant can be overwritten without warning, and it also applies to weapon oils and stones
- Enchanting an item in the trade window still asks

## 📍 Always on

**LFG player location:** the Looking For Group browse list shows each player's current zone next to the game type (the leader's zone for groups). No setup needed.

## 🧭 The Addon panel

Open it with the **minimap button**, **`/amity`**, or the **addon compartment** menu. Each module has its own tab with an on/off switch and its settings.
